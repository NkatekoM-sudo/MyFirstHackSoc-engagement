# CLD-0213: New OAuth consent: 'Bexley Sync Utility' granted by ewalsh.ext

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 4 · 2026-10-13
Verdict: True positive · Severity (my rating): Medium (reported Medium)
QA review: Satisfactory

## Alert
An application was given consent from ewalsh.ext@cloudora.io, at 23:52 UTC on Thursday

## Hypothesis
if malicious, there is still a malicious user with access to the guest account, and it giving access to a malicious application with three scopes, if benign, the consent was genuine for the project.

## Evidence checked
The proxy egress week1 log says that the redirect url was being redirected to 216 times after the consent was given, the alert tells us that the application ewalsh.ext@cloudora.io gave access with scopes Mail.read, files.read and offline access.

## Action
Escalated CLD-0213, as the evidence are telling us that the guest account can still be hosting an unauthorized user with stolen credentials, I recommend revoking the consent and adding a policy that revokes permission from granting scopes to unverified plublishers
