# CLD-0201: Repeated failed sign-ins: jt-admin@cloudora.io

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 1 · 2026-09-25
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
A Identity monitoring tool (Entra ID) sent out an alert for a series of repeated failed sign-ins: jt-admin@cloudora.io
, The attack was fired at 07:12.

## Hypothesis
if malicious, someone is brute forcing a freshly created admin account, if benign, the new starter is fighting temporary password.

## Evidence checked
Morning handover, I discovered there was a new IT engineer starting on Monday, and jtadmin provisioned Friday with a temporary password, helpdesk_week1.csv, as well as signin_week1.csv

## Action
Closed Reference HD-5121

## Justification
Every attack came from the office egress on a windows 11 build. HD-5121 logs Turner phoning the helpdesk about caps lock with the temporary password, and the sign-in log shows a first-try success at 07:22 after a verified reset. An attacker does not phone the helpdesk from inside the building
