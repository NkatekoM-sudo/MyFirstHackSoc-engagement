# CLD-0239: DMARC aggregate report: 1,430 failing messages claiming cloudora.io

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 10 · 2026-10-25
Verdict: True positive · Severity (my rating): Medium (reported Low)
QA review: Good

## Alert
There were 1, 430 messages claiming to be from  22 source addresses claimed to be from cloudora.io and failed both SPF and DKIM checks

## Hypothesis
if malicious, attacker is sending phishing emails or is impersonating Cloudora.io, if benign DKIM may not be configure correctly

the other 140 come from four different hosting addresses, coming from a spoofing source.
