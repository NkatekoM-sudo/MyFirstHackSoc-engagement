# CLD-0222: Reported internal email: 'Updated payroll portal' from ewalsh.ext

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 6 · 2026-10-21
Verdict: Escalate · Severity (my rating): High (reported Medium)
QA review: Good

## Alert
Three staff members received an email from ewalsh.ext@cloudora.io to re-authenticate their updated payroll portal at 21:14 UTC

## Hypothesis
if malicious, attacker is trying to gain staff portal login details by luring them to authenticate portals because of an update, if benign, the portal was updated and genuinely requires staff to re-authenticate

## Evidence checked
the internal phishing portal evidence validates that authentication was a success, so the message was deemed safe to open, but the message says following this weeks integration work the payroll portal has moved, but the link leads to an IP Adress that is on the watchlist, and the sign-in week2 says that Eleanor sent 12 recipients emails during the accounts session but the accounts IP address is also on a watchlist and the  handover says that once inside a tenant, the operator will send the next lure from that compromised account which I belieeve is the lure we are seeing on the alert.

## Action
Escalated because there was a successful authentication from the account with a potential phishing email sent to 12 staff memebers to gain access to their payroll portals.
